Roof ventilation

Attic and roof-space venting for Part 9 houses (BC Building Code, Section 9.19): how much vent area each roof needs, whether your vents are enough, and how many to add. A checking aid only: confirm results with the current code and the vent maker's label.

Settings

Vent area means net free area (NFA): the open area left after screens and louvres, printed on the vent or its box. The common vents below use typical values; replace them with your product's number.

Use the ceiling area from your floor plans

Uses the finished area of the top floor (the ceiling under the roof). The garage is left out; add it as its own section if its ceiling is insulated.
