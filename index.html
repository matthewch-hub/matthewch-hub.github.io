Spatial separation

How much glass each wall facing a property line may have, based on its limiting distance (the distance from the wall to the property line, or to the centre of a street). Built on BC Building Code Part 9, Subsection 9.10.15. A checking aid only: confirm results with the current code and your building official.

MC Designs · Code check

Roof ventilation

Attic and roof-space venting for Part 9 houses (BC Building Code, Section 9.19): how much vent area each roof needs, whether your vents are enough, and how many to add. A checking aid only: confirm results with the current code and the vent maker's label.

Settings

Vent area means net free area (NFA): the open area left after screens and louvres, printed on the vent or its box. The common vents below use typical values; replace them with your product's number.

Use the ceiling area from your floor plans

Uses the finished area of the top floor (the ceiling under the roof). The garage is left out; add it as its own section if its ceiling is insulated.

MC Designs · Energy

R-value calculator

Pick a pre-checked assembly or build your own layer by layer. The calculator works out the effective R-value the way BC Building Code Section 9.36 does (studs and joists lower the value of the insulation between them) and checks it against the minimum for your climate zone.

Where and what

Check your zone in the BC Building Code climate data (Table C-2) for your town. Minimums are from the BC house tables (9.36.2.6.-C and 9.36.2.8.-C, added in 2023 and the basis of the 2024 code). The 2024 code is free at bccodes.ca; if your city or Step Code report sets a different number, type it in “Own minimum”. Many houses instead show compliance with energy modelling under the BC Energy Step Code, so confirm the path with your building department.
